## 5. Internship Process (Real-Time Experience Program)

At Digital Brolly, internship is designed to make you industry-ready with real experience.

## BDLP - 1 Month Internship (With Placement Assistance)

- Type: Real-Time Practical Internship. Goal: Build strong foundation + basic experience.

- Tasks: Work on Real-Time Projects (Website optimization, basic SEO, social media posting). Hands-On Tool Practice (Google Ads basics, SEO tools, content creation tools). Guided Learning (Daily mentor support, task-based system).

- Outcome: Understand how real digital marketing works, gain basic practical experience, ready for internships & entry-level jobs. Includes Placement Support (Resume building, Interview preparation, Job assistance), but no guarantee.


## BDCP - 4 Months Internship (Job Guarantee Program)

- Type: Advanced Real-Time Internship. Goal: Make you job-ready with guaranteed placement.

- Tasks: Work on Client Projects (Handle real business clients, work on live campaigns). Run Paid Campaigns (Google Ads setup, budget management, performance optimization). Social Media Handling (Manage accounts, content planning, growth strategies). Advanced Tool Usage (50+ marketing tools, Automation, AI tools).

- Outcome: Real client handling experience, confident in interviews, 100% job-ready.

- Job Guarantee Program: Includes dedicated placement support, mock interviews, direct job opportunities, and a Placement Guarantee.

## Key Difference Between Internships (BDLP vs BDCP)

- BDLP: 1-Month duration, Beginner level, involves Basic Projects, provides Placement Assistance, results in being Entry-Level Ready. Best if your goal is just learning.

- BDCP: 4-Month duration, Advanced level, involves Client Projects, provides a Placement Guarantee, results in being fully Job-Ready. Best if your goal is a job with confidence.
